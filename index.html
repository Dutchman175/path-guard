Path Guard FAQ

Towers

Enemies

Grunt — weakest fodder. L1 Archer one-shots; soft vs Marksman/Fire. Green
Scout — fast, low HP. No immunities. Yellow
Skitter — quick pest, full damage from every tower. Lime
Tank — gray armored vehicle. Immune to Marksman and Elemental (use Gunnery). Gray
Brute — high HP, slow. Immune to Marksman — use Elemental or Gunnery. Orange
Raider — fast + tough. Sniper/Bazooka strong. Dark red
Titan — Wave 20 (1) & Wave 40 finale (2). Bigger gray Tank. Immune to Marksman and Elemental — use Gunnery. Gray

What to build vs enemies

Soft packs (Grunt / Scout / Skitter / Raider): any damage tower works.
Brute: Immune to Marksman only — use Elemental or Gunnery.
Tank / Titan (gray): Immune to Marksman and Elemental — Gunnery only.
Damage is binary: a tower either deals full damage or is Immune — no partial resists, no bonus damage.
Support: Barrier halts enemies on its path tile (more at once as it levels); Bank pays gold each wave clear.

How to play

Pick a map, place towers on green + tiles (build grid). Barrier goes on the path itself (amber tiles). Tap a placed tower to upgrade or sell (sell asks confirm). Double-tap a tower to upgrade fast.

Next Wave starts the next round.

Auto: On automatically starts the next wave.

Later waves get denser and tougher (especially around 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 35 / 40). Gold comes from kills and Bank income on wave clear.

Tips & extras

Hard Mode — denser spawns (toggle on Choose Map). Aim on a placed tower cycles First / Strong / Close. Speed 1x→2x→3x→5x.
