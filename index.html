What to build vs enemies

Packed foes — Sludge→3 Goo→3 Slime→3 Droplet; Wrecker→3 Mauler→3 Crusher→3 Brute; Colossus→3 Titan→3 Bulwark→3 Tank. Kill them and the smaller ones pop out. Each leaked body costs 1 life, including children.
Soft (green): Droplet / Slime / Goo King / Sludge — any damage tower works.
Orange (Brute / Crusher / Mauler / Wrecker): Immune to Marksman — use Elemental or Gunnery (Brute gate wave 12).
Gray armor (Tank / Bulwark / Titan / Colossus): Immune to Marksman and Elemental — Gunnery only (Tank at wave 30; nothing is immune to Gunnery).
Damage is binary: a tower either deals full damage or is Immune — no partial resists, no bonus damage.
Support: Barrier briefly halts enemies on its path tile (more at once as it levels; Bulwark, Titan, and Colossus walk through Barriers without destroying them); Bank pays gold each wave clear.

How to play

Pick a map, place towers on green + tiles (build grid). Barrier goes on the path itself (amber tiles). Tap a placed tower to upgrade or sell (sell asks confirm). Double-tap a tower to upgrade fast.

Next Wave starts the next round.

Auto: On automatically starts the next wave.

Pick a map, then Easy / Medium / Hard (40 / 50 / 60 waves). Clear Easy to unlock Medium; clear Medium to unlock Hard on that map. Difficulty only adds later waves (same enemies per wave number). Gold comes from kills and Bank income on wave clear.

Tips & extras

Difficulty — chosen per map after you pick it: Easy 40 / Medium 50 / Hard 60 waves. Clear Easy for Medium; clear Medium for Hard. Difficulty only adds later waves — Wave N has the same enemies on every difficulty. Daily is Hard length (60 waves) plus today’s modifier, and pays 25 Credits on clear. Aim cycles First / Strong / Close. Speed 1x→2x→3x→5x.

Upgrades — Credits, tower unlocks/caps, and perks (buy + equip) are saved in this browser only (local). Use Account → Reset to wipe progress and start over.

Quit this run?

You will lose this run and return to the menu.
